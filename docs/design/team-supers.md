# Team supers

What happens when both players in co-op fire their supers together: a combo attack made for each pair of soldiers.

**Status:** draft, 2026-10-15, waiting for the owner's read-through. The names and gags are first ideas, to rewrite in the crew's own voice.

**Read this when** you're building the team super, or a new soldier joins the crew and needs a combo with each of the others.

Up: [Design](README.md)

## How a team super works

From [co-op](player.md#co-op): when both players' supers are charged, they can fire them together for a screen-clearing team super, and each pair of soldiers has its own combo.

- **Firing it** (picked 2026-10-15): when both supers are charged and the pair has a combo, a TEAM button appears on both players' pads. Either player can fire it alone, and it spends both supers. SU still fires your own super as usual.
- **What it does** (owner, 2026-10-15): a short animation, two or three seconds, and then **every enemy on screen dies**. That's the rule for every team super; a combo can tweak it, but this is the outcome. Against a boss it lands a big hit but doesn't end the fight. It spends both supers.
- **Solo supers are different:** fired on its own, each soldier's super does its own thing ([kits](kits.md)), such as tones2's spew or kie's Steal, and isn't a screen clearer.
- **The look:** the action freezes for a beat, and a split card slams in with both soldiers' portraits and the combo's name, Street Fighter style, while the announcer calls "TEAM SUPER!". The combo then plays out across the whole screen, mixing both kits' effects.
- **Nobody is hurt by it:** like every co-op attack, it never hurts either player ([friendly fire is off](player.md#co-op)).
- **Two different soldiers** are always in co-op ([player](player.md#co-op)), so every pair below is two different crewmates.
- **No combo, no team super** (picked 2026-10-15): the TEAM button only appears for pairs whose combo is built. Other pairs fire their supers separately.

## Building them

- **Start with Sewer Rats** (tones2 + kie), the only pair co-op has after Mission one. Build the TEAM button and the split card with it.
- **Each later combo is built when its pair can first happen** (picked 2026-10-15), so a combo arrives with each newly freed crewmate.
- **Each combo reuses its pair's own kit pieces** (sludge, weasels, pills, waves and so on), so a combo is mostly choreography, not new weapons.
- **In co-op,** the world's authority runs the combo, like every attack that hits goons.

**Built** (2026-10-16): Sewer Rats and the TEAM button are in the game. TEAM shows on both pads (T on a keyboard, the left trigger on a gamepad) when both supers are charged and the pair is tones2 and kie; either player presses it and both supers are spent. The action freezes for the split card (the presser on the left, the partner on the right, "TEAM SUPER!" over the combo's name, and the announcer), then tones2's sludge floods the floor, kie's weasels surf the wave across the screen, and everything on screen pops; a boss loses 30% of its full health but always keeps a point. The goons hold still while it plays. Every screen plays the show and the authority deals the damage. The pairing and timings are `Core/TeamSuper.cs`, the show `TeamSuperShow.cs`, the card in `Ui/HudView.cs`. `?test=team` tries it solo, with a stand-in partner whose super is always charged (and yours refilled after each go).
