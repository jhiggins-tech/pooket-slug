# Pooket Slug design: history

What the owner decided in the first brainstorm (14 Oct 2026) that isn't in the other design documents: the Roguelite, which is on ice, and the ideas that were considered and not picked. It was kept in the root `DESIGN.md` until 16 Oct 2026, when the rest of that file had long since moved into this folder.

**Read this when** the Roguelite comes back, or you want to know whether an idea was already turned down.

Up: [Design](README.md)

## The Roguelite (on ice)
**On ice for now** (owner, 14 Oct): Missions come first. What was decided is kept here for when it's back.
- A separate mode of seeded runs. **A run is five stages**, with a crew tabk at the end of each, **then it loops** round at a higher alert, as in Risk of Rain. Depth keeps counting through the loops.
- **Coins**: goons drop them, and **between stages there's a shop** selling upgrade crates and gun crates, hearts and 1-ups. Whatever a run picks up, coins included, is gone when it ends.
- Any crew tabk can be a boss, tones2's included, and every tabk you beat is yours to ride for the rest of the run.
- **The alert level climbs as you go**, as in Risk of Rain: it goes up at each boss and every few minutes, so playing fast keeps it low. Each level brings more goons, faster bullets (fired more often, flying faster) and tougher tabks (more health, and an extra attack).
- **Co-op too, with one purse**: both players' coins go into it, and you agree at the shop.
- **Still open, for when it's back**: the shop's prices, how two players agree at it, whether beating a crew tabk in a run frees its pilot, and how often a run lets you call in a tabk.
- **Ranks when the Roguelite is back**: the plan was milestones for how far you get and the alert level you reach. Still open then: its milestone table, where depth keeps counting through the loops and the alert only climbs, so the two rise together and stalling to raise the alert needs thought.

## Considered, not picked
- **One-hit deaths**, as in Metal Slug: the owner pivoted to hearts.
- **PvP**: run-by-run duels on a shared seed, live versus, and rating players against the bosses. Ranks come from progress instead.
- **Other ways to play together**: passing the phone, relay turns online, and two players on one phone. Co-op is live online only.
- **Lasting growth**: a career shared with Tabks, and per-soldier mastery levels. Growth is crates within a mission (or run), and cosmetics from career XP.
- **Chosen alert levels**: DEFCON 5 → 1 picked before a run, or Hades-style twists. The alert climbs as you go instead.
- **More pixel terrain**: everywhere, or soft ground in levels. It's only in boss arenas.
- **Other run lengths**: three stages, or endless. It's five, then loop.
- **Between stages**: picking 1 of 3, a rest stop, or choosing the next boss. It's a shop.
- **Your tabk in Missions**: parked in the level, or chosen at the start. You call yours in.
- **The ability button**: holding the special, tapping your portrait, or swiping up. It has its own button.
- **Roguelite co-op**: own coins each, or solo only. It's co-op with one purse.
- **A dearer shop** as the alert rises.
- **Tabks' rank in Slug**: on its own, beside Slug's, or whichever is higher. It's Slug's.
- **Tabks' three starters** (tones2, kie and kcaj). It's tones2 alone.
- **More missions to start**: five, four then garyoldmancorp's, or five and a finale. It's one.
- **Other boss tabks**: the spike's boss restyled for each, or fighting in Tabks turns. Each fights with its own Tabks kit.
- **Which tabk comes**: always the newest, or a wheel. You choose before.
- **Mission one freeing** kcaj, ciarra or kiwicore. It frees kie.
- **Mission one elsewhere**: the servo car park, the Trollogram lab, or the suburbs. It's a military base.
- **A longer Mission one**: about 10 minutes, or 15+ with checkpoints. It's about 5.
- **Other goons**: ones that all blow up, Trollogram copies of the crew, or Trollogram mini-tabks. It's the spike's three, glitched.
- **kie's tabk with one move**: only the shell game, the weasel waves or the pickpocket. It cycles through all three.
- **Ranks for now**: mission feats, mission difficulty levels, waiting for the Roguelite, or showing your Tabks rank meanwhile. You climb slowly with stages beaten.
- **The call-in elsewhere**: a button that pops up when it's ready, or a tabk icon in the HUD. It's stick down + SP.
- **XP for feats**. It comes from clears, kills and freeing the crew.
- **Teaching tones2's kit** as you go, or in a training yard first. Mission one goes straight in.
- **Secret feats** for the hidden rungs, and **status-mod crates**. Both dropped (14 Oct).
- **Your tabk as artillery**, with a turret and charged shots. Only boss tabks fight that way (15 Oct).
