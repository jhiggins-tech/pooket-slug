# Mission two

Part of [the cutscenes](README.md), the one script for every scene: the rules, the format and every scene at a glance are there.

kcaj's mission, at the Brain Freeze creamery ([why the creamery](../story.md#mission-two-the-creamery)). It's the
first mission co-op can play, so every scene here plays on both phones in co-op. Drafted 2026-10-16; the story was
approved by the owner on 17 Oct, and the narrator lines are voiced (`voice/narration.json`).

## The Mission two briefing

Played when Mission two starts from the den, solo or in co-op, so a partner sees it too (owner, 2026-10-19). Until
then kcaj's string board glows in his dark corner to point the way ([the lobby](../lobby.md)). About 24 seconds, four shots; it can be skipped once seen. kcaj's string
board briefs it (owner, 2026-10-16): it stays up in his dark corner of the den before he's freed. **Who's in it:**
always tones2 and kie, whoever the players picked, since they're the only crew free at this point in the story; a
replay is the same. No intro.

1. INT. THE MOTOR POOL - MORNING                        5 s

The base, the morning after. The blast shutter is up and
the bay is empty. Tyre tracks and cyan scuffs lead out of
the back gate. TONES2 and KIE stand in the middle of it.

          By morning, the motor pool was empty.

                    KIE
          THEY TOOK THE LOT.

## kcaj's tabk arrives

Mission two's reveal, like [the motor pool reveal](mission-one.md#the-motor-pool-reveal) in Mission one (owner, 2026-10-19,
m2-finish: the intro should play as a scene that pushes in on the tabk, as kie's does). It plays the first time the
players reach the loading yard before the cold store, on both phones in co-op; the [boss intro](mission-one.md#the-boss-intro)
after it skips the roll-out and goes straight to the banner, the name card ("KCAJ'S TABK") and the music. Every later
fight, a replay included, gets only the boss intro. About 11.5 seconds, three shots; tap moves on, and it can't be
skipped the first time. Intro: Trollogram kcaj, shot 2. Set: the cold store (`coldstore`, its doors in the middle).

```text
1. EXT. THE CREAMERY, THE LOADING YARD - DAY            3.5 s

The players step in from the left. Ahead, the cold
store's big freezer doors. Frost creeps out along their
seams, and a stack of ice cream tubs beside them rattles
in time with an engine idling somewhere inside.

SFX: a deep engine idle; ice cracking.

                    NARRATOR (V.O.)
                    [m2-reveal-1]
          The cold store. Something in there is
          idling.

                                                   CUT TO:

2. EXT. THE LOADING YARD - CONTINUOUS                   5 s  

The doors fog open. KCAJ'S TABK rolls out of the mist, a
cone on its antenna, and stops. The camera pushes in on
the hatch. It pops, and up comes TROLLOGRAM KCAJ: kcaj,
but cyan and flickering, a cone in each hand.

SFX: the doors' hiss; the engine; the hatch; static.

          INTRO: TROLLO-KCAJ (builds as he rises, lands at
          2.5 s)

                    TROLLOGRAM KCAJ (GLITCHY)
          YOU'RE NOT IN MY NOTES.

                                                   CUT TO:

3. EXT. THE LOADING YARD - CONTINUOUS                   3 s

He slaps a sticky note on the turret, scrawled with a
red arrow pointing at the players, drops into the hatch,
and it slams. The turret swings round on to them.

SFX: the slam; the turret.

                    NARRATOR (V.O.)
                    [m2-reveal-2]
          Now they are.

                                INTO: THE BOSS INTRO, FROM
                                THE WARNING (control back)
```

**The boss intro after it** (in play, about 3 s): the screen locks, the siren, WARNING!, the announcer's "Warning!",
KCAJ'S TABK drops in under the banner with "Here comes the tabk!", and the boss theme kicks in on the tabk's first
shot. On a later fight the tabk rolls out of the fog in play first, as it used to, with no hatch and no intro.

## Freeing kcaj

After Trollogram kcaj is beaten on foot ([the pilot round](../tabks/bosses.md#the-pilot-round)), like
[freeing kie](mission-one.md#freeing-kie). About 15 seconds, five shots; it can be skipped once seen. Intro: kcaj, shot 2.
**Afterwards:** the next time the den loads, kcaj [moves in](den.md#a-crewmate-moves-in) to his nook on the upper floor and his corner lights
up.

```text
1. INT. THE CREAMERY, THE COLD STORE (IN PLAY)          3 s

TROLLOGRAM KCAJ freezes mid-taunt and tears apart, the
big version of the goons' death.

                    TROLLOGRAM KCAJ (GLITCHY)
          IT'S ALL CONNECT-

MUSIC: the boss music cuts out.

                                                   CUT TO:

2. INT. THE CREAMERY, THE COLD STORE                    4.5 s

The cold store's door creaks open, frost rolls out, and
the real KCAJ stumbles out in his own colours, rimed with
frost, holding an empty tub.

          INTRO: KCAJ

                    NARRATOR (V.O.)
                    [m2-freeing-2]
          And out comes the real kcaj. Slightly
          frozen.

                    KCAJ
          I'VE BEEN IN THERE FOR DAYS.

                                                   CUT TO:

3. INT. THE COLD STORE - CONTINUOUS                     2.5 s

He looks at the tub, then at the crew.

                    KCAJ
          I ATE THE EVIDENCE.

                                                   CUT TO:

4. INT. THE COLD STORE - CONTINUOUS                     2.5 s

TONES2 holds out a slushie. kcaj walks past it, opens a
freezer, and eats two cones at once.

                    NARRATOR (V.O.)
                    [m2-freeing-4]
          He's had a difficult week.

                                                   CUT TO:

5. INT. THE COLD STORE - CONTINUOUS                     7 s

kcaj points at the factory around them, then at the
crew. Nobody answers.

                    KCAJ
          I TOLD YOU. I TOLD ALL OF YOU.

                    TONES2
          OMFG.

KIE turns to tones2.

                    KIE
          LET'S PUT HIM BACK IN.

He sticks a sticky note on tones2's back.

                                          INTO: THE DEBRIEF
```
