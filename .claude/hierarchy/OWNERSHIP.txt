# ---------------------------------------------------------------- story
# The plot: what happens, in what order, and what each reveal is. A change to the
# story goes through this domain, because the plot's facts are spread across the
# missions, the crew roster and the scene list, and they have to agree.
#
# The plot of record is docs/design/story.md, and the plot in code is MissionStory.cs
# (which scenes a mission plays, in what order, and what is once-only). The other
# places the plot is expressed — the mission order in Missions.cs, the roster in
# Crew.cs, the scene names in SceneLibrary.cs — stay with their owners, and this
# domain's job is to keep them agreeing, which its consistency check does.
